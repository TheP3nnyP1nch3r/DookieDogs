How much does weekly service cost?

Price depends on how many dogs you have, your schedule, yard size, and current yard condition. Enter your details in the quote tool and we'll show your exact price on screen in about 60 seconds.

Why is a mobile number required to see my price?

We use your mobile number to save your quote, text you a copy, and help you finish setting up service if you have questions. Your price still appears on screen immediately. Dookie Dogs does not sell or share your number.

Is the first visit really free?

Your first standard visit is free when you start recurring weekly or biweekly service in an active service area. If the yard is three or more weeks behind or requires an initial reset, a one-time cleanup fee may apply. We will explain and confirm that fee before service begins.

What if the yard has not been cleaned recently?

Yards that are three or more weeks behind may need an initial reset before recurring service begins. The typical initial-reset fee is $50, but oversized or heavily accumulated yards may require a custom quote. Ty will confirm the amount before anything is scheduled or charged.

How does billing work?

Recurring service is billed per completed visit. Your quote shows the per-visit price before you enroll. Nothing is charged until your route and service details are confirmed.

Is there a contract?

There is no long-term contract. Service continues on the schedule you select until you pause or cancel. Pause or cancel before your next scheduled visit.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as the agreed service area is accessible and your dogs are secured according to your instructions, we can complete the visit while you are away.

What should I do with my dogs during service?

Keeping dogs inside gives the technician clear access to the yard. If your dogs may be outside, note that during enrollment so we can confirm the safest service arrangement.

What happens if the gate is locked or inaccessible?

We will contact you if we cannot safely access the agreed service area.

Do you take the waste with you?

Yes. Waste is double-bagged and removed from the property as part of the standard visit unless you request an approved alternative in your access instructions.

What happens during rain, snow, or severe weather?

We work in most weather. If ice, severe storms, deep snow, or unsafe yard conditions prevent service, we will text you with the delay or catch-up plan.

Which areas do you serve?

Dookie Dogs runs active routes in Bowling Green, Alvaton, Franklin, Auburn, Plano, Rockfield and Woodburn, KY. Enter your ZIP code to confirm current availability for your address.
